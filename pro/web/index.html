                      How the bot thinks

                      Every trading day after 15:20 IST the bot reads the completed daily chart, India VIX and the news, decides which market regime it is in, and follows that regime's playbook. Orders fill at the next morning's open.

                      RegimeHow it is detectedWhat the bot does
                      UptrendPrice above the 200-day average, 50-day above 200-day, ADX ≥ 20Buys pullbacks (RSI2 < 10), 55-day breakouts, or rides strong trends
                      TransitionTrend turning, averages not yet alignedOnly a confirmed 55-day breakout
                      RangeADX below 20: sideways marketStays in cash
                      DowntrendPrice and 50-day below the 200-day averageStays in cash (shorting lost money in 10-year tests)
                      StressIndia VIX ≥ 25 or in its top 15% of the yearStays in cash

                      The options trade

                      When the engine is long, the bot sells a bull put credit spread: it sells a put near 0.30 delta and buys a put 300 points lower, in the monthly expiry at least 28 days away. It collects premium, gains from time decay and from VIX falling in rallies, and its worst-case loss is fixed when it opens. It rolls to the next month with 7 days left and closes when the engine exits. Risk per trade is capped at 4% of the account.

                      Why not intraday options?

                      The old bot opened spreads after the morning move and closed them at 15:15 the same day. Spreads earn from time decay over days, so in a few hours they paid four legs of costs and earned almost nothing: 30% winners in its backtest. Of all option structures tested on 10 years of signals, only the positional bull put spread made money in both 2016–22 and 2023–26.

                      Learning and safety

                      • A walk-forward kill switch switches off any strategy whose recent record in the current regime turns clearly negative.
                      • News is a brake, not an accelerator: high negative news risk blocks new entries.
                      • Alerts fire for new signals, stop breaches, data or feed failures, stale data and large spread losses.
